frequently asked questions

How is this different from free BYOK AI tools?

Bring-your-own-key tools require your own Google AI (Gemini) API key and run entirely in your browser. Managed AI plans run on Sky Bridge's own infrastructure, with brand setup, usage tracking, and human support included.

What happens if I go over my monthly usage?

We'll notify you before you hit your limit. You can upgrade a tier or purchase a top-up block instead of being cut off mid-month.

Can AI Enterprise connect to our existing Odoo ERP?

Yes. Since Sky Bridge also implements Odoo ERP, AI Enterprise can be wired directly into your existing sales, inventory, or CRM workflows.

Is our data used to train external AI models?

No. Your content and business data are not used to train third-party models. Data handling details are covered in your service agreement.
